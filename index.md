# Signum Outdoors Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 2, 2026

Signum Outdoors ("Signum," "we," "our") makes an Android app that lets outdoor
groups (snowmobile, hunting, UTV, and overland parties) communicate and share
location over off-grid LoRa mesh radios when there is no cell service. This policy
explains what data the app handles and how.

## Summary

Signum is local-first. There is no Signum-operated server or backend in this
release, and the app does not upload your messages, group data, radio settings,
waypoints, or location to us or to any third party. Data you create in the app -
groups, messages, waypoints, saved radios, and settings - stays on your phone.

## What the app accesses, and why

**Bluetooth (scan and connect).** Used to discover and connect to your own
compatible MeshCore Companion radio over Bluetooth Low Energy. No Bluetooth data is
sent anywhere except that paired radio.

**Location (GPS).** Used to show your position on the group map, calculate
distance/bearing back to a saved waypoint, and - only if you turn on "Record my
track" - to record a local breadcrumb trail on your phone. Your phone's location is
never uploaded to us. It reaches other group members only when you explicitly send
it as a message or position over your own radio, exactly like any other message you
choose to send.

**Camera.** Used only to scan QR-code group invites. Camera images are never stored
or transmitted - only the text decoded from the QR code is used.

**Notifications and vibration.** Used to alert you to new group messages and to show
a status notification while the app is listening for messages in the background.
Both are covered by in-app settings you can turn off.

**Internet.** Used only to download optional offline map data (regional map files,
elevation tiles, and public land-boundary outlines) from public third-party map data
providers, at your request, for offline viewing. These are one-way downloads of
public map data - they do not send any of your data anywhere.

## What we don't do

- We do not operate a server that receives your messages, locations, groups, radio
  configuration, or channel secrets.
- We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
- We do not run analytics or advertising SDKs in this app.
- We do not require an account to use Signum.

## Your controls

You can clear your active group or all local Signum app data from within the app at
any time. Clearing app data does not change your radio's own channel configuration -
that is a separate, explicit action in the app.

## Changes to this policy

If Signum adds features that change this policy - such as user accounts, cloud
sync, or subscription billing - we will update this page before that feature ships.
